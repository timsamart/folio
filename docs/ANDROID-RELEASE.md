# Folio for Android

## Install

1. Open [the latest release](https://github.com/timsamart/folio/releases/latest).
2. Download the file ending in `-android.apk` and open it. Android may ask you to allow installation from the browser or Files app used for the download.
3. Choose **Files > your Markdown file > Open with > Folio**, or use **Share > Folio**.

Folio supports Android 7.0 or later. The APK bundles fonts, diagrams, code highlighting and mathematics for immediate offline use. Open UTF-8 .md, .markdown, .mdown and .txt files up to 2 MB each. Imported files are copied into the library; the originals are not modified or kept in sync.

The `.aab` asset is a store upload format; it cannot be installed by tapping it. Use the APK for a direct install. Check downloaded files against the release's SHA256SUMS file when needed.

## Update without losing your library

Download the newer APK and install it over the existing app. Keep a library backup first. Do not uninstall just to update: uninstalling removes the app's local data. Direct APK downloads do not auto-update.

The browser/PWA and APK have separate libraries. In the old installation, use **Document options > Back up library**, then **Restore library** in the other installation. Verify that documents arrived before removing anything.

## Read aloud

Tap **Read aloud**, select an installed offline voice, then **Choose a passage**. Use a paragraph's play button or the play button beside a heading in the outline. Paragraph playback ends at the paragraph; section playback includes its subsections and stops at the next equal or higher heading.

Android voice settings lets you manage your system speech engine and download language data. Quality depends on the installed voice. Playback pauses in the background, including screen-off use. Resume restarts the interrupted sentence. Folio does not include premium/cloud voices.

## Availability and support

The web app and direct APK downloads are free. The planned Google Play edition is a USD $2.99 one-time purchase, not yet published. No subscription or in-app purchases are included.
