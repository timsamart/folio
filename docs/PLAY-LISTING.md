**App name:** Folio — Markdown Reader

**Short description:** Read Markdown with diagrams, code, math, and on-device read aloud.

**Full description:**

Give your notes, essays, and technical documents a little room.

Folio turns Markdown files into a quiet reading experience with thoughtful typography, a personal library, and the details that make technical writing clear.

• Open Markdown from Android's file manager or share files into your library.
• Read Mermaid diagrams, highlighted code, and LaTeX mathematics offline.
• Listen to one paragraph or an outline section with an installed device voice.
• Pause, replay a passage, change speed, and return to your listening place.
• Find a phrase, jump through the outline, and bookmark documents worth keeping.
• Choose daylight, paper, night, or system colors and adjust text size and spacing.
• Export original Markdown and back up or restore your library.

Your library stays on your device. Folio has no account, advertising, analytics, or cloud voice service. Remote images load only when you request them.

Read aloud requires an installed offline voice from your Android text-to-speech engine. Quality and available languages depend on your device and downloaded voice data. Playback pauses when you leave Folio. Code, diagrams, and equations receive brief announcements instead of a spoken rendering of their source.

Folio opens UTF-8 .md, .markdown, .mdown, and .txt files up to 2 MB. Files are copied into your library; later edits to the original do not change that copy.
