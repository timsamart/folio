Your pages stay yours.

Folio 1.2 · Effective 13 September 2026

Folio is a Markdown reader maintained at timsamart/folio. It has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud synchronization, or paid voice service.

Your library

Documents, bookmarks, appearance settings, and reading and listening positions are stored locally in your browser or Android app. Files opened from another Android app are copied into Folio’s private storage until saved to its library. A failed save keeps the incoming copy so you can retry. Your original file is unaffected.

Folio does not upload your documents to its maintainer or GitHub. The Android app disables automatic cloud backup and device transfer of its data. You can export an original Markdown file or a JSON library backup to a location you choose. If that location is a cloud storage service, its policies apply.

Read aloud

The Android app uses the system text-to-speech engine and offers only voices that the engine reports as installed and usable without a network connection. It checks this again before speaking. The web app similarly offers only voices marked local by the browser. Folio does not call a cloud speech API or send text to a third-party voice service.

Your selected speech engine is separate software. Its accuracy in reporting offline voices and its own diagnostic or network behavior are controlled by that engine. You can choose and manage it in Android’s text-to-speech settings. Downloading language data through system settings may require a connection.

Connections you choose

The website and APK downloads are hosted on GitHub. Visiting them involves ordinary requests to GitHub under its privacy policy. Following external links, loading remote Markdown images, and exporting to a third-party document provider may contact those services. External Markdown images load only after you request them. Fonts, diagrams, code rendering, and mathematics are bundled locally.

Delete or move your data

Remove a document in its menu. Clearing the browser’s site data or the Android app’s storage removes its local library, settings, and saved positions. Uninstalling the Android app removes its local data. Keep an exported backup first. Exported copies remain wherever you saved them and can be deleted there. The browser app and APK have separate libraries.

Contact and changes

For questions, use Folio’s public issue tracker. Do not include private document contents in a public issue. Any future cloud voice feature will need a separate opt-in and an updated explanation before it processes document text.
