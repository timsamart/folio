# Quiet Leaves

Folio's store artwork carries the calm of a well-set page into a small, crowded storefront. Space is a material: generous margins, a measured baseline, and one clear reading direction make every element feel deliberately placed. The composition should remain legible before any fine detail is noticed.

The established ink blue and cool paper colors do the work. Pale layers suggest leaves of a document without imitating a phone or a new product interface. A restrained warm accent brings the human act of reading into the technical subject. Every curve and stroke receives the same care as a letterform.

Large serif type speaks first; a compact sans-serif signature supplies context. Diagram lines, code marks, and a small equation sit on the illustrated page as a single visual sentence. They describe the material Folio reads, with no claims about features beyond the shipped release.

Balance comes from a quiet block of language beside an airy page construction. Spacing is optically tuned rather than mechanically filled. The finished PNG should look resolved at a glance and reward closer inspection with consistent line weight, aligned edges, and carefully judged negative space.
